# Your Interview Guidance

For an existing idea, explore the problem and affected person, current workaround and cost, simple offer, rough economics and customer reach, fit with the owner's life, then evidence and one next learning test. Adapt this order to known answers and consequential gaps.

For no idea, use familiar work, observed problems, and reachable people to offer concrete possibilities early. Let the owner choose what to explore. Do not substitute an abstract life questionnaire, score, or startup pitch exercise.

Separate observations, assumptions, unknowns, contradictions, and missing evidence. Proposed test criteria should explain when to continue, revise, or drop this version. Never imply that an unperformed test succeeded or that a suggestion is an owner commitment.
