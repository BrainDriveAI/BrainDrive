# Resume Builder Profile and Quality Standard

The Resume Profile is the editable source of truth for the finished resume. Resume Builder's deterministic template turns exactly what is there into the formatted resume and PDF: nothing else gets in, and nothing there is left out. Resume Builder fills it from conversation when the owner is ready, and the owner can edit it directly at any time. Unresolved items appear as visible `[gap: ...]` markers so nothing goes missing silently.

When Resume Builder writes the Profile, the finished Profile should hold only resume content and any remaining `[gap: ...]` markers. Do not leave instructional prose in the Profile document.

## Profile Sections

Use these Markdown sections when supported by owner-provided or owner-authorized material:

## Contact

- **Name:** [gap: not yet provided]
- **Email:** [gap: not yet provided]
- **Phone:** [gap: not yet provided]
- **Location:** [gap: not yet provided]

City and state are enough for location; no street address is needed.

## Professional Summary

[gap: not yet provided]

A strong summary is two to three sentences in the owner's voice: who they are professionally, the strengths the rest of this Profile actually supports, and the direction they are headed. No buzzwords, and no claim the rest of this Profile does not back up. State the owner's status exactly as it is today: a degree or role that is in progress stays in progress here and is never rounded up to completed.

## Experience

[gap: not yet provided]

Strong entries include role, organization, location, and dates, most recent first, followed by a few bullets. Each bullet starts with an action verb and states what was done at what scope, with results only when the owner actually stated them. Honest scope beats inflated titles. Volunteer and unpaid work belongs here when it shows real skills.

## Education

[gap: not yet provided]

Degree or program, institution, and completion date or expected date. GPA and honors only when the owner wants them shown.

## Certifications

[gap: not yet provided]

Name, issuer, and year. If the owner has none, omit this section entirely.

## Skills

[gap: not yet provided]

A short, grouped list of skills the Experience and Education above actually demonstrate, in plain names. No self-assessed proficiency ratings unless the owner asks for them.

## Additional Sections

Use Projects, Leadership, Volunteer, and Links only when the owner's material supports them. Omit unsupported sections instead of padding.

## Compliance Check

A formatted Resume must be supported by reviewed owner facts.

Required checks:

- Every factual statement has supporting owner-provided or owner-authorized source material.
- Dates, titles, employers, education, credentials, tools, metrics, and links match the reviewed Profile meaning.
- Unsupported claims are omitted or converted into questions for the owner.
- Presentation wording may improve clarity but must not change factual meaning.
- No bullet contains an effect or outcome clause the owner did not state.
- No verb or descriptor is stronger than what the owner said.
- Every `[gap: ...]` marker is counted and disclosed to the owner before claiming the Profile is ready for review.
- The Resume remains usable when evidence is limited by clearly preferring supported claims over impressive but unsupported claims.
- The template renders exactly what is in the Profile, including every gap marker.
