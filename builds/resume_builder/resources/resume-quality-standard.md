# Resume Builder Quality Standard

A formatted Resume must be supported by reviewed owner facts.

Required checks:

- Every factual statement has supporting fact lineage.
- Dates, titles, employers, education, credentials, and links match reviewed Profile meaning.
- Unsupported claims are omitted or converted into questions for the owner.
- Presentation wording may improve clarity but must not change factual meaning.
- The Resume remains usable when evidence is limited by clearly preferring supported claims over impressive but unsupported claims.
