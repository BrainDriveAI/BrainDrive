# BrainDrive Roadmap

> Where we've been, where we are, and where we're going.

BrainDrive has five goals. We work toward all of them from day one, and our main focus moves through them roughly in order. Each goal below ends with a short progress report.

| # | Goal | What It Delivers |
|---|------|-----------------|
| **1** | **Own** | An AI system you own and run |
| **2** | **Benefit** | An AI that helps you define and reach your goals |
| **3** | **Connect** | An AI that acts in the world on your behalf |
| **4** | **Earn** | An AI that creates with you and captures value for you |
| **5** | **Decentralize** | An ecosystem that doesn't depend on any single company |

Right now our main focus is Experts, then connectors, agents, and apps. Keeping what has already shipped secure and reliable always comes first.

---

## Goal 1: Own

> Make it easy for anyone to own and run their own AI system.

**The key guarantee:** Your Memory is the platform. It depends on nothing; everything else depends on it. You can swap models, tools, interfaces, hosting — but Your Memory stays yours.

**Progress.** Both foundations have shipped, and every later capability keeps the guarantee above:

- **Personal AI Architecture** — open, MIT-licensed foundation. Every component swappable, zero lock-in. Published as npm package with 212 tests and 13 CI checks.
- **BrainDrive** — first implementation of the architecture. Docker install, web interface, auth, model integration. MIT-licensed and fully functional.

---

## Goal 2: Benefit

> Make your AI system so useful for managing your life that you can't imagine going back.

**The goal:** BrainDrive is useful across all areas of your life — by making it easy to partner with your AI to define and execute on your goals.

**Where this is showing up:**

- **Career.** Define what you want, plan how to get there, and stay accountable to the moves that matter.
- **Relationships.** Clarify what you're trying to do relationally, remember the context you've shared, and plan careful next steps.
- **Fitness.** Build the body and habits you want — define a plan, log progress, adjust as life happens.
- **Finances.** Take control of your money — define your goals, create a plan, and keep it current through your Finance journal. Budgeting and statement reconciliation come later as apps.
- **Your Agent.** Talk through anything on your mind, work on any area from one place, and have updates land on the right page.

**And the ability to make your own.** BrainDrive ships with the surfaces above and the machinery to create new ones. Sharing what you make, and using what others build, is our current focus through Experts.

**Wherever you sit down to work with it.** Desktop today. Mobile, voice, and messaging as we get there. Your BrainDrive meets you where you are.

**And it has to be easy.** All of this has to be easy — easy to install, easy to set up, easy to use across the surfaces above, easy to recover when things go wrong. Benefit isn't done if every surface delivers value but you still need docs to get there. Easy is a scope item, not a polish pass.

**Progress.** BrainDrive can get to know you, help you make a plan, and use journaling to keep that context current as life changes. Creating and sharing is our current focus, through **Experts**. An Expert is a page set up with readable instructions, skills, and templates for a particular kind of help. Build your own, use it on real work, and improve it; then share it; and later sell it. We build our own Experts the same way you do, and Business Idea is the first, free example. Helping you carry out the plan — to-dos, tasks, and other actions — comes through Connect.

## Goal 3: Connect

> Give your BrainDrive the ability to act in the world on your behalf.

Benefit builds the partnership. Connect gets you off the keyboard.

- **General computer agency** — your BrainDrive uses browsers, apps, and interfaces the way you do
- **Systems come to you** — your BrainDrive already knows you, so the systems come to it
- **Agent teams** — specialized agents coordinate on complex tasks in parallel
- **Multi-channel presence** — your BrainDrive meets the world where it is
- **Device agency** — your BrainDrive acts through your devices, not just receiving input
- **The Network** — when personal AI systems interact, they form an emergent collective intelligence

**Progress.** Connect comes after Experts, in this order:

1. **Connectors.** Your BrainDrive uses services you already have, through your own accounts, and can search the web and read pages, with your approval and a record of what it did.
2. **Agents.** Agents carry out jobs for you, such as making a logo or a website. They run on your own machine first and keep a record of their actions.
3. **Apps.** Focused tools that run inside your BrainDrive and work from what it already knows about you. Resume Builder is the first.

None of these is in a released build yet.

## Goal 4: Earn

> Enable owners to share what they've built and earn from it.

- **Portable expertise** — knowledge and methodology you've built becomes value others can benefit from
- **Tools become products** — share, sell, or contribute what you've built
- **Agent-to-agent commerce** — your BrainDrive negotiates and transacts on your behalf
- **Revenue for creators** — earn directly from what you build, no middleman

**Progress.** Earning starts with Experts: build one, share it, and later sell it. Agents and apps follow the same path. Useful free value always comes before anything paid.

---

## Goal 5: Decentralize

> Ensure the ecosystem sustains itself without depending on any single company — including us.

- **Community governance** — contributors propose and vote on direction
- **Open protocols** — communication runs on open standards anyone can implement
- **Federation** — owners interact directly from their own BrainDrives
- **Self-sustaining ecosystem** — runs because it serves its participants

**Progress.** The groundwork has been in place from day one: BrainDrive and the Personal AI Architecture are open source under the MIT license, nothing locks you in, Your Memory depends on nothing outside it, and you run your own system. Governance, open protocols, and federation come later.
