# BrainDrive Roadmap

Our mission is to build a superior user-owned alternative to Big Tech AI systems.

There are five major areas we believe we need to execute to do so:

1. **Own** — Build an AI system that is owned and controlled by the user, instead of a Big Tech company.
2. **Benefit** — The more you use your AI system, the better it should get to know you and the more powerful it should become.
3. **Connect** — Your AI system should act in the world on your behalf.
4. **Earn** — Your AI system should create with you and capture value for you.
5. **Decentralize** — The user-owned AI ecosystem should not depend on any single company.

Work overlaps, but we are working through each area in rough order.

---

## 1. Own

> Make it easy for anyone to own and run their own AI system.

**The key guarantee:** Your Memory is the platform. It depends on nothing; everything else depends on it. You can swap models, tools, interfaces, and hosting, but Your Memory stays yours.

**Progress:** We have developed and released the [Personal AI Architecture](https://github.com/Personal-AI-Architecture/the-architecture), an open, MIT-licensed blueprint for building personal AI systems without lock-in.

And we have developed and released BrainDrive, the first implementation of the architecture: an MIT-licensed personal AI system designed to help you define and reach your goals. It is available as Mac and Windows desktop apps and as a Docker install, and you can easily switch between cloud and local AI models.

**Still to do:** Ensure that agentic and other capabilities can be added without giving up ownership and control over your AI system.

---

## 2. Benefit

> Make your AI system so useful for managing your life that you can't imagine going back.

**Progress:** BrainDrive gets to know you, helps you define your goals and make a plan, and uses journaling to keep that context current as life changes. It ships with pages for Career, Relationships, Fitness, and Finances, plus Your Agent, which lets you talk through anything and work on any area from one place. You can also create your own pages for anything the defaults don't cover.

**Still to do:**

- **Experts.** Make it easy to create, use, and improve an Expert: a page set up with readable instructions, skills, and templates for a particular kind of help. Then make it easy to share Experts, and use ones others have built. We build our own Experts the same way you do; Business Idea is the first, free example.
- **Easy everywhere.** Keep BrainDrive easy to install, set up, use, and recover without reading documentation.
- **Wherever you are.** Desktop today; mobile, voice, and messaging as we get there.

---

## 3. Connect

> Give your BrainDrive the ability to act in the world on your behalf.

**Progress:** We have defined the open contract that apps use to run inside BrainDrive. None of the capabilities below is in a released build yet.

**Still to do,** after Experts and in this order:

1. **Connectors.** Your BrainDrive uses services you already have, through your own accounts, and can search the web and read pages, with your approval and a record of what it did.
2. **Agents.** Agents carry out jobs for you, such as making a logo or a website. They run on your own machine first and keep a record of their actions.
3. **Apps.** Focused tools that run inside your BrainDrive and work from what it already knows about you. Resume Builder is the first.

Longer term: your BrainDrive uses browsers and apps the way you do, specialized agents work together on complex tasks, it meets you on the channels and devices you use, and it connects with other personal AI systems on terms you set.

---

## 4. Earn

> Enable owners to share what they've built and earn from it.

**Progress:** Not started for owners yet. The first step is selling Experts.

**Still to do:**

- **Sell what you build.** Experts first, then agents and apps, always with useful free value before anything paid.
- **Portable expertise.** Knowledge and methods you've built become value others can benefit from.
- **Agent-to-agent commerce.** Your BrainDrive negotiates and transacts on your behalf.
- **Revenue for creators.** Earn directly from what you build, with no middleman.

---

## 5. Decentralize

> Ensure the ecosystem sustains itself without depending on any single company, including us.

**Progress:** The groundwork has been in place from day one. BrainDrive and the Personal AI Architecture are open source under the MIT license, nothing locks you in, Your Memory depends on nothing outside it, and you run your own system.

**Still to do:**

- **Community governance.** Contributors propose and vote on direction.
- **Open protocols.** Communication between personal AI systems runs on open standards anyone can implement.
- **Federation.** Owners interact directly from their own BrainDrives.
- **Self-sustaining ecosystem.** It runs because it serves its participants.
